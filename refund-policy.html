Refund Policy

Our approach to cancellations, rescheduling, and refunds.

Back to Home Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Overview

We want every client to feel confident booking a program with PureHarvest Nutrition. This Refund Policy explains how cancellations, rescheduling, and refund requests for consultations, meal plans, and packages are handled.

2. Consultation Cancellations by You

  • Cancellations made at least 24 hours before a scheduled consultation are eligible for a full refund or credit toward a future session, at your preference.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before a session may be eligible for a credit, but are generally not eligible for a cash refund, except where required by law.
  • No-shows without prior notice are not eligible for a refund or credit.

3. Cancellations by Us

If PureHarvest Nutrition cancels a scheduled consultation or session for any reason, you will be offered a full refund or a credit toward a future session, whichever you prefer.

4. Meal Plans & Multi-Session Packages

Because meal plans are prepared specifically for each client, requests for a refund must be made within 7 days of purchase and before the plan has been delivered. Unused sessions in a multi-session coaching package may be refunded on a pro-rated basis within 14 days of purchase, provided no more than one session has been used. After that window, packages become non-refundable but may still be eligible for a credit at our discretion.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a cancellation, rescheduling, or refund, please contact us directly by phone — we do not currently process these requests through an online form. Call +1 (000) 000-0004 (placeholder — add your number) or visit us at 19 Garden Row, Market District (placeholder — add your address), and our team will process eligible requests within 5–7 business days.

6. Processing Time

Approved refunds are returned using the original payment method where possible and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your payment provider.

7. Exceptions

Promotional offers, trial consultations, and gift purchases may carry their own specific refund terms, which will be communicated at the time of purchase and take precedence over the general policy above.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy periodically. The version posted on this page at the time of your booking will apply to that transaction.
